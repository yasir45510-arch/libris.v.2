The LIBRIS Reading Room

Ada arrived at the LIBRIS Reading Room on a quiet Monday morning. She wanted to learn Python so she could build a helpful reading assistant for students.

The librarian, Mr. Okafor, showed Ada how to organise books by title and author. He explained that a good digital library should remember where each reader stopped.

Ada wrote a small program that saved a reader's paragraph position. When Ben returned the next day, the program welcomed him back and continued his story from the exact paragraph he had reached.

At the end of the week, Ada asked the assistant, "Why should a reading application save progress?" The assistant found Mr. Okafor's explanation and replied that saved progress helps a reader return to the correct place without searching through the whole book again.
